\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Recent advances in neural machine translation have substantially improved translation quality. This improvement mostly benefits high-resource languages. Low-resource languages such as Ruund remain largely underrepresented. This gap is due to the scarcity of parallel corpora and digital language resources. This thesis addresses this gap. It investigates machine translation approaches tailored to low-resource settings. To this end, a neural translation system was developed for the Ruund-French language pair.

To tackle this challenge, a parallel corpus was compiled: \textbf{OpenRuund}. It was built from New Testament texts and poetic texts, yielding a total of 8~431 sentence pairs. After digitization and optical character recognition, the data were cleaned, normalized, and aligned. Three neural translation models were adapted to Ruund through \textit{fine-tuning}: mBART-50, MarianMT, and NLLB-200-distilled-600M. Performance was assessed using BLEU and chrF metrics. This automatic assessment was complemented by human evaluation, conducted by native Ruund speakers and a specialist in African linguistics.

The results show that pretrained models can produce meaningful translations despite the limited amount of training data. NLLB-200-distilled-600M achieved the best overall performance in both translation directions. For French$\rightarrow$Ruund translation, it obtained a BLEU score of 38.82 and a chrF score of 58.10. For Ruund$\rightarrow$French translation, it obtained a BLEU score of 24.11 and a chrF score of 54.80. This model was integrated into a web application. The application also includes a collaborative module for collecting textual and spoken data. All produced resources, including the corpus and trained models, were released. This release supports scientific reuse and contributes to the development of natural language processing tools for Ruund. To our knowledge, this work constitutes a first for Ruund: the first publicly available neural machine translation system and the first publicly available parallel corpus for this language.
